What is Digital Districts?
An always-on digital operating model designed to reach up to 10× more HCPs than face-to-face alone — converting non-visited HCPs into prescribers or qualified F2F targets through a scalable digital front door. Launched first for Mounjaro, followed by Foundayo.

Consents Bulk Upload

Consents Reconciliation
Two regional solutions are actively being explored to address gaps in HCO address data:

• IQVIA HCA API — evaluated for automated reconciliation of non-reconciled records; go-live targeted by end of July
• Google Maps API — complementary data enrichment for hospital address data gaps; currently in POC stage — reach out if you would like to be part of it
• Gulf & ANZ affiliates have additionally introduced automation and AI-assisted initiatives to accelerate gap resolution in their markets.
WhatsApp Consent New Policy
A separate, dedicated consent for WhatsApp is now mandated by Global Legal / GPO. Key points for affiliates:

• Affiliates may initiate local consent collection using existing local process and storage now
• Timelines for GCCP and global platform readiness will be communicated as confirmed
• An assessment is underway to evaluate integration of GCCP with local WhatsApp vendors and Sprinklr to ensure compliant opt-out handling across channels

Lilly Passport
Passport dropout rates remain a friction point under active resolution. IBU Central is focused on both customer experience improvements and underlying platform fixes.

Good practices for consent collection beyond Passport have been shared by Germany, France, UAE, and Canada. IBU is currently reviewing these approaches and will share best practices soon across affiliates.
Unified HCP Experience (UE) Milestone
The first HCP brand pages for Foundayo will go live in UAE in July 2026. This marks a significant milestone:

• First implementation to include individual tracking via WebSDK
• UAE learnings will be used to assess the impact on customer journey quality and inform wider rollout
For UE and landing page queries: Contact Pedro Asensio.
SFMC Landing Pages Interim
As an interim solution until full Unified Experience is available, IBU is building a standard template for SFMC landing pages. In parallel, the team is evaluating Adobe as a future hosting platform for greater readiness and governance.
Adobe & True Personalization

Global has approved a pilot of Adobe Journey Optimizer (AJO) for UAE, KSA, and Canada in 2026, paving the way for omnichannel personalisation at scale in these markets.
